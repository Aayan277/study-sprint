# Study Sprint: what serious students want next

I read the README, PLAN.md and the code, and checked each idea with grep so that none of them is already built. I did not edit any files. One caveat on evidence: Reddit hardly ever showed up in search results. Most of the evidence below comes from the Anki forums, official help pages for the apps, and review sites, not from first-hand Reddit threads.

Fits = whether it works in a static PWA with no server and no AI running on a server.

## Feature ideas, in priority order (★ = top 5)

1. **★ Study by tag or question ID** (medical, law). *Problem:* med students keep the AnKing deck suspended and unsuspend only the cards tagged with the UWorld question IDs they missed. A paid add-on exists just to do this ([AnKing Step 2 guide](https://studycardsai.com/blog/how-to-use-anking-for-step-2), [Atlas add-on](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/new-add-on-atlas-uworld-results-to-anking-cards-resources/70098)). Study Sprint can only start a session for whole decks. *How:* paste a list of question IDs or pick a tag, then choose "Unsuspend these" or "Study these now". **M. Fits.**
2. **★ Personal scheduling tuned to your history** (everyone). *Problem:* the app uses the standard FSRS settings for everyone (`js/srs.js`). Anki users tune these to their own review history, and the forums treat that as an important step ([FSRS forum](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/optimizing-fsrs-settings-for-high-stakes-neet-ug-2027-exam-11-month-timeline/69981/4)). *How:* an "Optimise" button in Settings runs the official optimiser inside the browser ([fsrs-browser](https://www.npmjs.com/package/fsrs-browser)). **M. Fits.**
3. **★ Audio on cards, and keep the sound from Anki decks** (language). *Problem:* sound is removed when an Anki deck is imported (`js/format.js:115`), and language decks depend on native audio ([Migaku guide](https://migaku.com/blog/japanese/anki-language-learning-guide)). *How:* store sound files the same way pictures are stored, with a play button and an auto-play option. **M–L. Fits.**
4. **★ "Elements" cards for rules** (law, also medical lists). *Problem:* bar students need to recall the elements of a rule, not word-for-word text ([JD Advising](https://jdadvising.com/four-tips-for-memorizing-for-the-bar-exam/), [law student on the Anki forum](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/how-to-memorize-short-lists-3-8-items/5514)). *How:* the card shows the rule's name, you recall the elements one at a time and tick the ones you got, and the card is rated by how many you missed. It can reuse the step-by-step card code. **S–M. Fits.**
5. **★ Cards that work in both directions** (language, law). *Problem:* Anki's "Basic (and reversed)" notes come in with only the front-to-back card (`js/anki.js:194`), and you can't make a reverse card yourself. *How:* a "Both directions" switch makes a second linked card with its own schedule, using the same rule as cloze cards (one card per note a day). **M. Fits.**
6. **Listening mode for cloze cards** (language). *Problem:* Clozemaster's most popular mode is hearing a sentence and then filling in the blank ([Clozemaster FAQ](https://www.clozemaster.com/faq)). *How:* the device voice the app already uses reads the sentence out, and you type the missing word. **S. Fits.**
7. **Turn a reading into cards** (language). *Problem:* LingQ's main idea is that you tap unknown words while you read and they become cards ([LingQ review](https://www.fluentu.com/blog/reviews/lingq/)). *How:* paste an article, tap words, and each one becomes a cloze card using its own sentence. You type the meaning yourself or use the existing "Make cards with Claude" prompt. **M. Fits.**
8. **Marking typed rule answers by key words** (law). *Problem:* the letter-by-letter check punishes a rule that is correct but worded differently. *How:* mark key words in the answer (for example `[[intent]]`), and a typed answer is marked on how many of them it includes. **M. Fits.**
9. **Missed-question log** (medical, law). *Problem:* the main way students learn is "do a question block, then make cards from what you missed" ([UWorld and Anki guide](https://studycardsai.com/blog/how-to-use-uworld-and-anki-for-step-1)). *How:* add the question ID, topic and why you missed it in a few seconds. Each entry becomes a tagged card, and Stats shows your weakest topics. **M. Fits.**
10. **Postpone** (everyone). *Problem:* Anki's most-used scheduling add-on (FSRS Helper) offers "postpone" for trips and busy weeks, and it is desktop-only ([forum](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/native-advance-and-postpone-options-for-ankidroid-ankimobile/53349)). The app already lets you review ahead but can't push cards later. *How:* push the cards that suffer least from a delay back by N days. **S. Fits.**
11. **Furigana (readings over kanji)** (language, Japanese). *Problem:* the app comes with JLPT kanji decks, but readings can't be shown above the kanji, and shared Anki Japanese decks write them as `漢字[かんじ]`. *How:* show that pattern as small text above the kanji, with an option to show it only on the back. **S. Fits.**
12. **Your own glossary** (medical, law). *Problem:* AMBOSS's Anki add-on is popular because it underlines terms and shows a definition when you hover ([AMBOSS](https://www.amboss.com/int/anki)). *How:* define a term once, and it is underlined on every card with a pop-up definition. **M. Fits.**
13. **Record yourself and compare** (language). *Problem:* the device voice can read a card to you, but there's no way to practise saying it. *How:* record yourself, then play your recording straight after the device's version. Nothing is saved. **S. Fits.**

**Why these five:** 1 and 2 decide whether medical students stay or go back to Anki. 3 and 5 are the most basic needs for language learners. 4 is the clearest thing that would set the app apart for law students.

## Improvements to existing features

1. **Anki cloze notes lose their field names** (`js/anki.js:182`). Every field except the first is joined into one block of unlabelled text. AnKing notes have many fields (First Aid, Sketchy, lecture notes and more), so this becomes a wall of text. Keep each field's name as a heading and fold the section away.
2. **Sound is dropped without saying so.** Import already warns about extra pictures it skipped. Show "N sounds not imported" the same way.
3. **Play has no "I was right" button.** A typed answer that was really correct but worded differently is marked wrong, and that counts as Again on a due card. Add an override and "Accept this answer from now on". WaniKani users rely on exactly this ([user synonyms](https://knowledge.wanikani.com/account-and-membership/wanikani/user-synonyms/)).
4. **Tags are flat and lowercased on import.** AnKing's grouped tags (written with `::` between levels) become long single strings. Show them as folders in the Tags tool in Browse. This also supports idea 1.
5. **Exam readiness is only an estimate from the standard settings.** Until idea 2 exists, add a short note that readiness is an estimate. Once it exists, update readiness using the tuned settings.
