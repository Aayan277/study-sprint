# Changelog

One entry per release, newest first. Up to v4.58 these are the detailed notes that used to live in PLAN.md; from v4.59 they are the short notes from `js/whats-new.js` (the app shows those under Settings → About → See what’s new). Versions before v4.8.1 have no entry here; see docs/plan-history.md.

- **v4.70:** Clearer headings and help buttons that line up. On a computer, Settings has a section list at the side, Play rounds fill the screen like Review, and buttons on Review show their name and keyboard shortcut when you point at them. The time on Start says “about 17 min” instead of “~17 min”.
- **v4.69:** Course code and Exam date now say they are optional when you make or edit a deck.
- **v4.68:** Anki import shows one line saying what comes along, with Change to see the options. Once installed, the app can open Anki files offline after it has been online once. Add exam date on Today is one button, and searching by tag on Decks now counts matching cards.
- **v4.67:** Searching on the Decks screen now finds cards too, and deleting a deck offers to save a copy first. On a computer: buttons line up in two sizes, Start sits at the right of the top card, and switches are on the left. The card filter’s flag option is now called Flagged, and your own model now needs stronger proof before it takes over.
- **v4.66:** Search in Help, and a Paste Claude’s answer button in Import. Undo for deletes in the hard-cards coach, and tidier end screens. Shorter lines on Today, Stats and decks.
- **v4.65:** Undo after deleting cards, a deck or an import. Settings, Add a card and Practice test are shorter: extra options sit under More. A + button on phones to add cards, bigger tap areas, and less text on screen.
- **v4.64:** On a computer, Tab now reaches Review first on a deck page, and your own model always gives Hard, Good and Easy different gaps.
- **v4.63:** New in Advanced scheduling: you can load your own model file. It watches FSRS first and only takes over once it does better. Easier taps on a phone, the Review button comes first on a computer's deck page, and Clinical shows upcoming exams again.
- **v4.62:** Calmer screens: only the main button is coloured, and Decks takes less space on a phone. Fixed: the card no longer jumps on a phone when you flip it, and the streak shows a snowflake only when a freeze was used.
- **v4.61:** A calmer study screen: plain rating buttons, and the question stays put when you flip. Today shows how long your reviews will take, and exam cards are simpler.
- **v4.60:** A cleaner look: no more glows, calmer borders and corners. Studying cards is more focused: bigger text, taller rating buttons, and on a computer the side menu hides until you finish.
- **v4.59:** Learning cards are no longer shown early when they come before your reviews.
