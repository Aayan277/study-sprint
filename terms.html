A free hobby project

Study Sprint is made and run for free by one student developer. It is provided as it is, with no warranty. I try hard to keep it working, but I can't promise it will always be available or free of mistakes, and, as far as the law allows, I can't be held responsible for lost data or for anything that goes wrong from using it.

It may change or stop

Features may change, and the app or the sync service may stop one day. I'll give reasonable notice where I can. You can always use Settings → Your data → Export backup to take everything with you.

Keep your own backups

Export a backup of anything important now and then, and keep it somewhere safe. Syncing is not a backup: if you delete something on one device, it is deleted everywhere.

What you may upload

  • Only upload cards, pictures and sounds that are legal and that you have the right to use.
  • Don't use the service to harm others or to store anything illegal.

Don't abuse the service

  • No bots or scripts to create accounts or hammer the server.
  • Don't try to fill the storage or to reach other people's data.
  • Each account has storage limits. They are shown in Settings, and they apply to everyone.

I may remove accounts that abuse the service.

Your data stays yours

Your cards belong to you. You give me only the permission needed to store them and show them back to you. More in Privacy.

Contact

Changes to these terms

If the terms change in a way that matters, I will update the date at the top, and where I can, say so in the app.
